Jay Hesselberth is a Professor of Biochemistry & Molecular Genetics at the University of Colorado School of Medicine and a member of the RNA Bioscience Initiative. His lab focuses on understanding protein translation through the lens of transfer RNA (tRNA), applying nanopore sequencing technologies to study dynamic tRNA usage, and developing RNA therapeutics for pediatric genetic diseases.
